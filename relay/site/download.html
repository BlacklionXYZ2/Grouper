Download Grouper

Install the app on the computer that will be your hub, then add your other devices. Builds come from the releases page. The newest successful build is the pre-release called “Latest build (harness)”.

Windows

Installer for 64-bit Windows: Grouper-windows-x64-setup.exe

The installer is unsigned, so SmartScreen shows a warning. Choose More info, then Run anyway.

Get the Windows installer

Mac

Mac app for Apple Silicon: Grouper-macOS-arm64.zip

The app is ad-hoc signed, not notarized. Unzip it and move Grouper.app to Applications, then run:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Grouper.app

Or open System Settings, then Privacy & Security, and choose Open Anyway.

Get the Mac app

iPhone

The iPhone app isn't on the App Store or the releases page yet. iOS apps need Apple signing, so for now install it from Xcode, using the project on GitHub.

In your browser

Once your hub is linked, the web app works on any device with a browser, with nothing to install.
