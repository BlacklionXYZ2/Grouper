Many models, one place

Chat once and let Grouper pick the right model for each job, or start work threads that run in parallel. Bring your own accounts: OpenRouter, Anthropic, OpenAI, or models running on your own machine.

Your devices, one hub

The hub runs on your Windows PC or Mac. Your iPhone, your Mac and the web app all talk to it, so a conversation started on one device continues on the next.

Keys and chats stay with you

Provider keys are encrypted on your hub, and chats are stored there too. There is no Grouper cloud copy, no analytics and no tracking.

Reach it from anywhere

Sign in with Google and the relay connects your phone to your hub from any network, with no ports to open. It only passes requests along. It doesn't store your messages.

Grouper is open source. You run your own hub.
