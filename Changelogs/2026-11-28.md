- **Why:** this is the relay `docs/relay-design.md` describes. It replaces the owner's Cloudflare tunnel. Choices the spec left open:
  - Times in tokens and nonces are unix seconds.
  - Unknown `/relay/v1/*` and `/relay/auth/*` paths return 404 JSON instead of being forwarded.
  - The sign-in page is served with status 200.
  - Idle detection runs from a 60 s alarm, so a silent hub is closed 90-150 s after its last frame rather than exactly at 90 s. A tighter alarm would stop the object hibernating.
  - Extra close codes 4002 (hub forgotten) and 4003 (idle); client sockets get 1012 when the hub goes away.
  - The platform auto-answers the exact text `{"t":"ping"}` on every socket, including client sockets.
  - `cf-*`, `x-real-ip`, `forwarded` and spoofed `x-forwarded-*` headers are dropped before forwarding.
- **Verified:** `npm install`, `npx tsc --noEmit` (clean), and `npx wrangler dev --local` starts. `node test/smoke.mjs` passes 72 of 72 checks. It covers per-account routing isolation, nonce reuse, wrong or tampered nonce, wrong aud, expired, stale or future iat, wrong iss, unverified email and bad signature (all refused), credential stripping, streaming, `err` before and after `res`, WebSockets (text and binary), sign-out everywhere, re-claim closing the old socket with 4001, DELETE hub, and 503 for a user with no hub.
- **Open:**

  - Nothing deployed; no Google OAuth clients created; no `RELAY_SECRET` set anywhere.
  - Not tested: the 100 s response timeout, the 90 s idle close, bodies near 16 MiB, hibernation of the Durable Object (local workerd rarely hibernates a short test).
  - The browser sign-in nonce is not bound to the browser (the spec's design). A login-CSRF hardening, such as a short-lived cookie set at `/relay/auth/start`, is possible later.
