Grouper privacy policy

Last updated 26 November 2026

Grouper is software you run yourself. Each person who uses it runs their own Grouper hub on their own hardware (a computer, a virtual machine or a server they control) and connects their own devices and apps to it. This page explains what a hub stores, what leaves it, and what the Grouper project itself sees.

What your hub stores

  • Your chats, the work threads they start, their results and files, and the settings you choose.
  • The devices you connect and their tokens (only a hash of each token's secret).
  • The API keys you add for AI providers, encrypted with a key kept outside the hub's database.
  • If you sign in with Google: your Google account's unique id, email address and name, so the hub recognises its owner.
  • If you turn on notifications: the address your browser gave for delivering them.

All of this stays on the hub you run. The Grouper project has no copy of it.

What leaves your hub

  • AI providers you configure (for example OpenRouter, OpenAI or Anthropic) receive the messages and files your chats and threads send them, under your own account with them and their privacy terms.
  • Google, when you choose "Sign in with Google": you sign in on Google's own page, and Google tells your hub who you are (id, email, name). Grouper asks only for these basic profile details, never for access to your Google data such as Gmail or Drive.
  • Browser push services (run by your browser's maker), if you turn on notifications: they carry a short notice that a tool is waiting for your approval, with the tool, device, thread and chat names. Notifications never include the command, its contents or your messages.

What the Grouper project sees

Google only returns sign-ins to addresses registered in advance, so browser sign-ins pass through a fixed return page at grouper.clockmillbarn.uk, which immediately forwards the result to your own hub. That page runs in your browser and stores and logs nothing. Apart from this, the Grouper project receives no data from your hub or apps, and Grouper contains no analytics, advertising or tracking.

Your control

  • Your hub's owner can delete chats, revoke devices and sessions, remove API keys and turn off notifications at any time.
  • You can unlink your Google account in the hub's Settings, or remove Grouper's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Deleting the hub's data folder removes everything it stored.

Contact

For a hub someone else runs, contact that person. For the Grouper apps and sign-in, use the support email shown on Grouper's Google sign-in screen.
